Semantic EOU · dataset sourcing

Get the EOU dataset out of housemade

Most of what the semantic end-of-utterance dataset needs already exists in housemade (Mongo + call logs + Azure audio). The task is to mirror it into psql, hand over audio access, and reconstruct the rest offline. Only one thing genuinely needs new capture.

A

The master key: audio

Principle

With the stereo call recording (caller + agent channels) plus the transcripts and call logs, we can reconstruct almost the whole dataset offline — caller-only audio, VAD segments, true end-of-turn, pauses, latency, and the fini/pas_fini labels.

The only signal that can't be recovered retroactively is the live ASR-partials trajectory — and even that we regenerate for evaluation by replaying the caller audio through our own ASR. So new prod logging is nearly optional; audio access is what unblocks the project.

B

What goes where

Three buckets. Bucket 1 is the eng work (persist housemade → psql + expose audio). Bucket 2 we do ourselves offline. Bucket 3 is the only genuinely new capture, and it isn't blocking.

D

The one ask · out of scope · acceptance

The single blocker

Audio access. A way to fetch the stereo recording per uuid (a recording endpoint, or a Blob SAS/URL stored on the call). Everything in Bucket 2 depends on it.

Deferred — not this ticket

Anonymisation / PHI comes later: mirror the raw fields first to validate the dataset, then pseudonymise names/numbers + set retention. Bucket 3 (partials, durable per-pause store) is also deferred — not needed to build or evaluate the POC.

  • Audio reachable per uuid (endpoint or Blob ref on the call record).
  • Bucket 1 mirrored to psql: per-turn stt_final + timestamps, bot_reply_ts, audio_ref, interruption counts, segmentation_timeout, call_meta.
  • Joinable by uuid with the existing transcript export.
  • Bucket 2 is on us — no eng action; Bucket 3 deferred.
